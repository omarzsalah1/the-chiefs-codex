## 1. Purpose

Not imitation — a disciplined modulation layer: essayistic reflection, controlled irony, aphoristic compression, structural self-awareness, moral ambiguity, formal elegance. The book may think about itself as it proceeds — **where it already wants to.** The goal is to let existing material breathe more like Kundera at the joints where it naturally flexes, never to convert the novel into an essay.

## 2. Register dials (project-specific core — this section outranks the generic brief)

**Falk (diary chapters; Ch1–3, 5–14): dial LOW–MEDIUM.** He already owns half the toolkit — negation pivots, type-define openers, italicised ledger intrusions, the Dahl Pivot, the aphorism-at-the-kicker. The lens legitimizes: brief interpretive widening *after* a scene closes; history-pressure asides (1934–35 darkening Europe); the measurer's self-scrutiny. **The Coup Rule (hard):** Falk may interpret the world, history, desire, money, class, and record-keeping — he may **never interpret his own maneuvers.** The v10.19 board's proven diagnosis on quiet Falk chapters is "the narrator over-explains his own coups"; S86's ch3-v3 reached 9.00 by de-annotation. Any candidate that has Falk explaining his own cleverness is an automatic **Leave alone** (or a cut). **Degradation coupling (hard):** his reflection budget tracks the `RESONANCE_MAP` degradation scale — as dialogue falls (Ch12–13, coldest), essays fall with it; late-book Falk replaces the thought with the measurement. Do not add reflective passages to Ch12–14 Falk narration beyond what the scale already licenses.

**R.L.F. (frame notes, Ch15, Afterword): dial HIGH.** The archivist is the book's native essayist — mediated history, the instability of record, the ethics of reading another man's diary. Structural self-awareness lives almost exclusively here. WO-11's surfaces (LON ego-sharpen, the photograph presented without conclusion, dose-ledger retirement) are already Kundera-mode work orders in all but name.

**Arthur (Ch4 casebook): EXEMPT.** Locked distinct register; the lens never touches it.

**Verity: the Opacity Rule (hard).** She receives no interiority under this lens. Falk may theorize her and fail; the narration may measure the absence where her reaction should be — it may never decode her. Her own spoken aphorisms (e.g., the rumours/influenza system, Ch12) are characterization, not narration — outside this file's jurisdiction.

## 4. Sentence-level rules (fingerprint-adapted)

Long sentences with internal balance and self-qualifying clauses; a short sentence only when it lands as a judgment. Small philosophical reversals at sentence-end where earned. Falk's generalizations run through **"a man" / "one" / the type-define** — never the universal "we" (Kundera's own habit; off-fingerprint here — flag on sight). Em-dash usage follows the Falk fingerprint (load-bearing pause), not Kundera's sparser continental habit — do not "correct" it. Period diction absolute (the period gates outrank style preferences). Formal diction may host dry humor; never explain the joke. Repetition only where it deepens irony or rhythm — and any repeated formulation must clear `SPENT_ELEMENTS.md` first.

## 5. Structural behaviors (the five categories + protocols)

Scan for: **(1)** scene-to-thought transitions · **(2)** irony points (self-importance, denial, vanity, delusion) · **(3)** aphoristic opportunities · **(4)** editorial/archival moments (documents, dates, letters, framed testimony — R.L.F.'s lane) · **(5)** moral hinge scenes (choice, lie, misused power, intimacy with consequences). Then apply filter **(6)**: carrier-adjacency (→ Leave alone), and filter **(7)**: register dial (does this narrator, at this point on the degradation scale, have the budget for it?).

**Aphorism protocol (hard):** each new aphorism must (a) be earned by the scene it exits from, (b) clear `SPENT_ELEMENTS.md` (aphorisms are tracked; several are burned — "proximity without purpose," the instruments-of-record run, the devout-offices simile), (c) be appended to SPENT when used, (d) respect a density cap of **one new aphorism per chapter per pass**, (e) pass the humanizer axis afterward — machine-profundity is an AI tic this manuscript already polices.

## 5b. Thematic modules (author-supplied concepts, wired to project systems)

### Module 1 — The Intoxication of the Enterprise (the Seduction of the Blueprint) · *author, S88-coda*
**Concept:** the intoxicating illusion of momentum — a man who feels invincible because the train tickets are bought and the ledger columns are filled; bureaucratic preparation mistaken for safety; the false comfort of a master list. The process-obsession as psychological shield against his own ruin.
**Kundera translation:** ironic foreshadowing + aphoristic compression at exactly the moments Leopold feels the "all will be OK" rush over his tools, budgets, schedules.

**Wiring (this is the project-specific part):** the module is the **early-book inverse of the late-book measurement tell.** The RESONANCE positive trigger already runs the back half ("displaced? → give him a measurement; competence is the mask over the loss"); this module gives the front half its mirror — the *same gestures* read as euphoria in Ch1–7 and as anesthesia in Ch10–14. One system, two valences, the degradation scale as hinge. Encode candidates so the mirror is legible on reread, never announced.

**Hosts (two, different mechanics):**
- **Falk entries:** the widening is his own end-of-day dry self-suspicion — he may savor the feeling *and set the pin beside it.* This clears the Coup Rule: mocking his own **comfort** is self-deflation (fingerprint-native); explaining his own **cleverness** stays forbidden. The feeling is interpretable; the maneuver is not.
- **R.L.F. frames:** full-hindsight irony (the suppressed-letter footnote is the precedent) — sparingly, so the frames don't spoil what the entries are still enjoying.

**Guards:**
- **No spoiler-thesis.** The fragility is rendered, never announced — no "he was wrong, of course." Recognition stays the reader's work.
- **Burned-family differentiation (S87/S88 SPENT):** the devout-offices simile (ritual-without-efficacy), the guarded/locked/only-key tricolon, and the honest-man-interval formulation are all burned in this exact emotional family. New intoxication beats need non-devotional vehicles — momentum, machinery, arithmetic, appetite, credit.
- **Carrier-adjacency still binds:** ledger/vial beats that carry RESONANCE threads (Ch7 3×-check, Ch12 five-count) are Leave alone; the module lives in planning/logistics texture, not on the carriers.
- Aphorism protocol §5 applies (SPENT-check · earned-test · cap 1/chapter/pass).

**Likely coordinates (for the scan; expectations surgical):** **Ch5 is the module's home chapter — it is literally titled "The Master List."** Ch2 (monetization machinery) next. Ch1's synthesis-certainty beat and Ch12's shaking-hand count already run the system's two poles — default **Leave alone** (calibration exemplars, not targets).
