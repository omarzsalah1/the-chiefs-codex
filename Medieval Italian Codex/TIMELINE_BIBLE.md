## 3. Conflict register — live text vs. locked anchors

**TB-1 · Federico's death is four years early. (LOAD-BEARING — biggest single restructure.)**
Live: Ch13 (1478–79) stages Federico dying ("with the duke dying," Orsini/Rinaldi regency struggle, "when he passes… young Guidobaldo will need guidance"); Ch19's canon chronology reads the 1482 entry as *the prince's* illness ("Bertoldo refuses to rule while the prince is in the shadow of death") and lists 1478–1480 as "height of Bertoldo's competence; temptation to rule."
Lock: Federico dies at Ferrara, September 10, 1482 (Directive Stage 1; Stage 8 explicitly: "Verify that Federico does not die in Urbino or in 1478").
Ripple: Ch13's entire regency-crisis premise re-dates 1478→1482; Ch14's span compresses; Ch12's 1482 scene re-reads; Ch15's opening 1482 illness material re-anchors; Ch19 chronology entries for 1478–1480 and 1482 rewrite; Prologue's *Spring 1483* frame becomes months-after-the-death (this actually strengthens it).
Resolution path: Stage 5 Pass B ("rebuild Federico's final 1482 sequence around Ferrara"). **D-1 CONFIRMED by Omar 2026-07-10 — the lock governs; rebuild scheduled.**

**TB-2 · Federico titled "Duke" from 1461. (Mechanical, pervasive.)**
Live: zero uses of "Count"; "Duke/Duke Federico" ~128× across Ch1–11 (+2 in front matter), all in years 1461–1473.
Lock: Count until 1474.
Rule to apply: Count in all scenes dated pre-1474; Duke thereafter. Watch dialogue-vs-narration and the elevation itself (a scene opportunity Pass A may want).

**TB-3 · Guidobaldo's eighteenth birthday sits in the wrong chapter-year.**
Live: Ch15 stages "Guidobaldo's eighteenth birthday… feast of San Sebastiano" (Jan 20) — the protected birthday-toast invisibility scene — inside a chapter spanning 1482–1487. Ch18 separately has him "eighteen now" in spring 1490.
Lock: b. Jan 1472 → 18th birthday = January 1490. Two consistent fixes: (a) make Ch15's scene his **sixteenth** birthday, January 1488 — weeks before the Elisabetta wedding, dovetailing with Stage 3's instruction to fold the public name-omission into the 1488 wedding orbit; or (b) keep it the 18th and relocate the scene to the Ch18/1490 court occasion. **Decision D-2.**

**TB-4 · Guidobaldo absent before Ch13.**
Live: zero mentions in Ch1–12. With death moved to 1482, the heir (b. 1472) should exist in the book's world from Ch11–12 (1473, 1475, 1482 scenes). Light seeding only — a birth notice, a glimpse — not new plot.

**TB-5 · Three directive-required persons have zero footprint.**
Ottaviano Ubaldini: 0 mentions (required for the succession guidance, Stage 1/5C). Elisabetta Gonzaga: 0 (Stage 4 requires informed political agency, her own network, capacity to govern in 1494, and discovery of the concealment). Battista Sforza: 0 (Pass A integration). All three are new construction under the Addition Rule (1,000 new displaces 1,500 old).

**TB-6 · Caterina is a payoff without a fuse.**
Live: Caterina appears only in Ch15 (5) and Ch17 (1) — her returned-money scene and death land without the "letters, missed visits, deferred obligations" the directive requires seeding across the book (Stage 4). Seeding sites available in Ch2–12 (Fano references exist in Ch1, 2, 6).

**TB-7 · Minor, verified consistent.**
